Mark Tracker Privacy Policy

How the Mark Tracker app handles location and service-visit recordings on company-owned devices, consent, and your data.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Mark Tracker is an app published by Mark The AI Office Manager, LLC ("Mark AI," "we," "us"). It is used on company-owned devices that field-service businesses issue to their technicians. It does two things for the business that operates the device: (1) it shares the device's work location during the workday, and (2) during on-site service visits, a technician can record audio of the visit so the business can review the visit and coach its technician. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

Who the app is for

Mark Tracker is set up by a business on a device it owns and issues to a worker for work use. It is not intended for personal devices or personal use. The worker turns location tracking on from within the app and grants permission before any location is shared, and starts a visit recording only when they choose to.

Information the app collects

  • Precise location — GPS latitude and longitude, accuracy, and (where available) heading and speed of the device, with timestamps. Collected only while location tracking is turned on in the app.
  • Service-visit audio — when a technician records a service visit, the app captures audio of that visit, which is uploaded to the business along with a text transcript and coaching scores generated from it.
  • Technician identifier — the worker/technician ID the business assigns to the device.
  • Device identifier — used to associate location reports and recordings with the correct device.

The app does not collect your contacts, photos, or browsing activity, and it does not record audio except during a service visit a technician chooses to record. It does not use your information for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service-visit audio recording

During an on-site service visit, a technician using a company device may record audio of the visit. The recording is uploaded to the business and used to review the visit and to coach the technician — for example, on how clearly they explained the work and the estimate. Recording happens only during a service visit a technician starts; the app does not record continuously or in the background outside of a visit.

Recording a visit can capture the voices of the technician and of other people present, such as a customer. The business that operates the device is responsible for notifying everyone present that the visit is being recorded and for obtaining any consent required by law. Recording and consent laws vary by location, and some states require that all parties consent to audio recording before it begins.

How the information is used

  • Location — to show the business where its technicians are during the workday, for dispatching, scheduling, routing, and operations, and on that business's own dashboard.
  • Service-visit recordings and transcripts — to let the business review a visit and coach the technician on their customer visits.

How the information is shared

  • With the business that operates the device — location and visit recordings are visible to the business that set up the device, which is the worker's employer.
  • With service providers that host the service and transcribe audio on our behalf, under contracts that limit their use of the information to providing services to us.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

Your control

  • Location is shared only while tracking is on; you can stop it at any time by tapping Stop Tracking in the app.
  • A visit is recorded only when a technician starts a recording; it is not automatic.
  • You can turn off location or microphone access for Mark Tracker at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security.

Data retention

Location reports are kept on a short, rolling basis — automatically deleted after approximately 48 hours. Service-visit recordings and transcripts are retained only as long as the operating business needs them for review and coaching and as provided in our agreement with that business, and are deleted on the business's instruction.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access restricted to the operating business and authorized service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Mark Tracker is a workplace tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@markaioffice.com, Mark The AI Office Manager, LLC.

Mark Tracker is intended only for devices owned by a business and used for work. The business is responsible for informing its workers and anyone recorded during a service visit, and for obtaining any consent required by applicable law.
