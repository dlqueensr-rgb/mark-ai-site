Quoting, selling & memberships

Priced off your numbers — never ours.

"We don't use the pricing in ServiceTitan" is the most common objection we hear. Good — neither does Mark. He uses the hours on each job, then runs your own math.

✓Your formula: (your hourly rate × the job's hours) + your service fee. If a job isn't in your book, Mark uses the average pro time for your area and runs the same math.
✓Materials folded in live from the customer's local stores, with your markup applied — a real total, not labor-only.
✓Your rules, automatically: markup tiers, van-stock %, card fees, service fee.
✓Memberships sold & e-signed on the call — residential and commercial — then auto-scheduled across the year.
✓Handles objections like a closer — reframes price around the monthly payment and membership savings.
