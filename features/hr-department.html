H.R. is the job most owners do last — until it's urgent.

A write-up that never got documented, a handbook that's years out of date, a missing safety plan — these are the things that hurt when an employee dispute or an inspection comes. Most shops are too small for an H.R. person and too big to wing it.

How it works

  • Write-ups you approve, following a ladder you can edit: coaching, verbal, written, final, then termination review.
  • State-specific forms, employee handbook, injury and illness prevention plan, and workplace violence plan.
  • Employees sign by text, and everything lands in a digital employee file.
  • Callback tracking, quarterly reviews and awards, leave tracking, and your OSHA log.
  • Recruiting: Mark screens applicants by text, runs the phone interview, and sends you the ones worth meeting.
You stay the decision-maker on every write-up and every hire. Mark makes sure it's done right and on paper.
