You're paying for hours you can't verify.

Timesheets get tapped late, forgotten, or padded. Spiffs get calculated by hand. State meal and rest rules are easy to miss until there's a claim. Payroll is one of the biggest checks you write — and the one most owners review the least.

How it works

  • ServiceTitan timesheets are checked against tablet and van GPS and each job's location, with billable and non-billable time separated.
  • CSR and tech spiffs are calculated and added automatically.
  • Office staff clock in through ServiceTitan or Mark; sick days fill in on their own; everything runs in each office's own time zone.
  • Your state's rules are shown with the hours, and meal and rest premiums are flagged for review.
  • You approve, and the hours go to ADP. Records are kept for years.
Every hour Mark checks is an hour you know you actually owed — and a cleaner record if anyone ever asks.
