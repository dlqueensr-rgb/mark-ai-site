SMS Privacy Policy

How Mr. Handyman of Scottsdale handles text messaging, consent, and opt-out.

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Mr. Handyman of Scottsdale may send service-related SMS messages including appointment confirmations, technician arrival notifications, service reminders, and follow-ups regarding active service requests.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP.

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How customers opt in

Customers opt in to receive SMS messages by:

  • Submitting a service request form on our website and providing their mobile number.
  • Verbally providing consent during a recorded service booking phone call.
  • Consent is documented in our CRM system with timestamp and source.

For general data privacy practices, please refer to the Neighborly Privacy Policy.
