Busy isn't the same as profitable.

A job can look great on the invoice and still lose money once you count the extra hours, the materials nobody turned in a receipt for, and the card fee. Without real job costing you can't tell which work to chase and which to price up.

How it works

  • Labor time is verified by GPS, not just what was tapped in the app.
  • Card fees and materials are counted against the job they belong to.
  • Jobs missing receipts are flagged — and show up in your morning briefing until they're closed out.
  • Mismatches between tapped time and GPS time are called out so you can see where hours go.
  • It works alongside your ServiceTitan reports — it doesn't replace them.
