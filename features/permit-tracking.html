Open permits cost you twice.

A permit nobody followed up on means a job that can't close, an inspection that never got scheduled, and an invoice you can't collect. In a busy shop it's easy to lose track of which jobs are waiting on the city.

How it works

  • You set, per office, who pulls permits and who schedules inspections — Mark follows your process.
  • Status updates come from one-tap buttons on your dashboard, or your tech can simply text Mark from the job.
  • Mark keeps everyone on the right next step and flags permits that have gone quiet.
  • Optional: hold the final invoice until the inspection passes, so you never bill before the job is truly done.
