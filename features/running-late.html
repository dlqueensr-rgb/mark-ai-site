A late tech isn't the problem. Silence is.

Customers forgive a tech who's running 40 minutes behind. They don't forgive sitting at home wondering. Your office usually finds out late, then spends the morning on the phone apologizing — Mark handles it before the first angry call comes in.

How it works

  • Mark sees when a tech is running behind on the day's schedule and texts the next customer a friendly heads-up with an updated window.
  • If the customer can't wait, they just reply — Mark offers new times and moves the appointment in ServiceTitan.
  • When a tech calls out sick, Mark works through that tech's whole day: reassigns jobs to qualified techs who are open, or reschedules the customers who need a new time.
  • You get a text summary of what changed, so nothing on the board surprises you.
A two-line text at the right moment turns "they never showed" into "they kept me in the loop" — and that's the review you actually get.
