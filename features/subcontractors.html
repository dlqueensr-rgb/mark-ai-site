A non-compliant sub on a job is your risk.

If a sub's insurance lapsed last month and they get hurt on your customer's property, that's your problem. Keeping five documents current for every sub — and remembering which sub does which work — is exactly the kind of tracking that slips.

How it works

  • Five compliance documents tracked with expiration dates: general liability certificate, workers' comp, state license, W-9 and signed agreement.
  • Mark won't dispatch a sub unless every document is current — and reminds subs before anything expires.
  • Your pricebook decides which sub handles each job type.
  • Mark books the ServiceTitan job under your subcontractor business unit, with no tech seat needed.
  • The sub texts Mark "done" when the work is finished, and payment follows your net-30 terms.
