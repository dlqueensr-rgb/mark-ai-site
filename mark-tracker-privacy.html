Mark Tracker Privacy Policy

How the Mark Tracker app handles location on company-owned devices, worker consent, and your data.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Mark Tracker is an app published by Mark The AI Office Manager, LLC ("Mark AI," "we," "us"). It is a workforce location app installed on company-owned devices that field-service businesses issue to their technicians, so the business can see its own technicians' work location during the workday. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

Who the app is for

Mark Tracker is set up by a business on a device it owns and issues to a worker for work use. It is not intended for personal devices or personal use. The worker turns tracking on from within the app and grants location permission before any location is shared.

Information the app collects

  • Precise location — GPS latitude and longitude, accuracy, and (where available) heading and speed of the device, with timestamps. Collected only while tracking is turned on in the app.
  • Technician identifier — the worker/technician ID the business assigns to the device, so the business knows which device's location it is seeing.
  • Device identifier — used to associate location reports with the correct device.

The app does not collect your contacts, photos, messages, or browsing activity, or any data unrelated to work location. It does not use location for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How the information is used

  • To show the business that operates the device where its technicians are during the workday — for dispatching, scheduling, routing, and day-to-day operations.
  • To display location on that business's own operations dashboard.

How the information is shared

  • With the business that operates the device — location is visible to the business that set up the device, which is the worker's employer.
  • With service providers that host and operate the service on our behalf, under contracts that limit their use of the information to providing services to us.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

Your control

  • You can stop sharing location at any time by tapping Stop Tracking in the app.
  • You can turn off location access for Mark Tracker at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.
  • When tracking is off, the app does not collect or share location.

Data retention

Location reports are kept on a short, rolling basis — automatically deleted after approximately 48 hours — so the business can see recent movement and when a technician left a job site. We do not keep a long-term history of a worker's location.

Security

Location data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access restricted to the operating business and authorized service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Mark Tracker is a workplace tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@markaioffice.com, Mark The AI Office Manager, LLC.

Mark Tracker is intended only for devices owned by a business and used for work. The business is responsible for informing its workers that the device shares location during the workday and for obtaining any consent required by applicable law.
